# KOS-003 — Career North Star

## Vision

To become a world-class builder of Data Intelligence Systems that help people and organizations make better decisions using data, artificial intelligence, and reliable research.

---

## 1. If I become world-class at one thing over the next 10 years, what do I want it to be?

Designing Data Intelligence Systems that combine data science, AI agents, automation, research, and software engineering to solve complex real-world problems.

---

## 2. What kinds of problems do I want my work to solve?

Problems where better decisions can be made through data.

Examples include:

- Healthcare
- Education
- Business
- Government
- Scientific Research
- Finance
- Climate
- Personal Productivity

---

## 3. Who do I ultimately want to help?

From individuals in my local community to organizations and governments around the world.

My goal is to create technology that scales from helping one person to helping millions.

---

## 4. Looking back in 10 years, what evidence would convince me that I built a meaningful career?

- Built an organization solving meaningful global problems.
- Published influential research.
- Created open-source tools used worldwide.
- Helped students and researchers.
- Built products that improve decision making.
- Mentored future builders.
- Left behind systems that continue creating value long after I built them.
