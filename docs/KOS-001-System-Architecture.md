# Vision

Build a Personal Intelligence System that helps me continuously learn, think, research, build, make better decisions, and create value for the world.

---

# Mission

To design a system that transforms knowledge into action through research, engineering, automation, and continuous improvement.

---

# Engineering Principles

## 1. Build Before Consuming

Every learning session should produce something.

---

## 2. Everything Connects

Knowledge should never exist in isolation.

Projects, papers, people, skills, opportunities, and ideas should connect together.

---

## 3. Systems Over Goals

Design systems that naturally produce results instead of chasing results directly.

---

## 4. Document Everything

If it is worth building, it is worth documenting.

---

## 5. Automation Over Repetition

If something is repeated often, design a better process.

---

## 6. Kaizen

Improve a little every day.

---

## 7. Evidence Over Assumptions

Research first.

Decide second.

---

## 8. Build for Future Kingsley

Every decision should make Future Kingsley stronger than Present Kingsley.
